# 10 Objection Framework

**Purpose:** Surface and answer the ten objections every prospect runs silently
before buying. Each answer should be **grounded in the Review Research (01) data**
— real complaints and real desired results — not in assumptions.

> DO NOT TAKE THIS LIGHTLY. We're touching the core foundations of the business —
> where the product fits the market and how to speak to the customer through the
> funnel.

---

## Answer all 10 questions

| # | Question | Your answer (grounded in real data) |
|---|----------|--------------------------------------|
| 1 | How is your product different from everything else your customer has seen? |  |
| 2 | What's in it for me? What result(s) will this product give me? |  |
| 3 | How do I know this product is real? |  |
| 4 | What has held me back in the past from getting the results I want? |  |
| 5 | Who/what is to blame for my past lack of results? |  |
| 6 | Why should I do this now? |  |
| 7 | Why should I trust you? |  |
| 8 | How does your product work? |  |
| 9 | How can I get started? |  |
| 10 | What do I have to lose? |  |

## How each maps downstream

| Objection | Feeds |
|-----------|-------|
| 1 differentiation | USP, Mechanism |
| 2 results | Benefits, Promise |
| 3 proof | Claims & Proof |
| 4 past failures | Justify failures (Blair Warren) |
| 5 blame | Anti-avatar / common enemy |
| 6 urgency | Reason-to-respond-now |
| 7 trust | Credibility, proof |
| 8 mechanism | Unique Mechanism |
| 9 onboarding | CTA / next step |
| 10 risk | Risk reversal / guarantee |

Ten data-grounded objection answers that feed the Campaign Promise (05) objection
