# Dream Customer ICP — Ideal Client Profile Worksheet

**Purpose:** Define the dream customer with enough specificity that copy, creative,
and targeting all connect with the same person. Vague ICPs make marketing
expensive and disjointed.

**Instructions:** Be as specific as possible and list AS MANY attributes as you
can. Leave a field blank rather than guessing — blanks are research gaps to fill,
not invitations to invent.

---
