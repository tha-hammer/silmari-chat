# One Belief Statement

**Purpose:** Distill everything into a single sentence the prospect must believe to
buy. It fuses the **New Opportunity**, the **Customer's Key Desire**, and your
**Unique Mechanism**.

> DO NOT TAKE THIS LIGHTLY — this is the core of the sales message.

**Pattern:**
> *[Key Desire] is the key to [Outcome], and it's only attainable through
> [Unique Mechanism].*

---
