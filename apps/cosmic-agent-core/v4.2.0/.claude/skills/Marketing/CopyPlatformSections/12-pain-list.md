## Methodology

Sales copy is "fractal" in nature. The "your appeal" section gives 4 categories of appeal. Each appeal will generate different "mechanisms", "claims and proof", different obstacles for the "what stops them" section. Your job is to reason through these dependencies and produce the actual text, not just a high level description of what might be done. 
CORE FRAMEWORKS:

1. FRACTAL AIDA PATTERN
Each level of writing must follow the AIDA pattern (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action):
- Individual sentences contain micro-AIDA
- Paragraphs contain sentence-level AIDA
- Sections contain paragraph-level AIDA

2. EMOTIONAL-LOGICAL BALANCE
- A (Attention): 80% emotional appeal, 20% logical setup
- I (Interest): 60% emotional hook, 40% logical foundation
- D (Desire): 40% emotional amplification, 60% logical justification
- A (Action): 90% emotional urgency, 10% logical reassurance

ADVERTISEMENT SPECIFIC GUIDELINES:
- Each sentence must be self-contained yet connected
- First sentence: Pattern-interrupt + emotional spike
- Middle sentences: Problem-agitation + solution-hint
- Final sentence: Clear, urgent call-to-action
- Use sensory and emotional language
- Create immediate relevance

## Framework

Dimensionalized pain points are based on what your prospects are experiencing. For ease, write them out in the form of a question.

Also think about any "trigger moments." One time, a prospect told me the reason she decided to lose weight is because her daughter hopped on her lap and said "mommy you're squishy."

## Instructions

Use this checklist section to devise different dimensionalized pain points. Use the actual checklist to devise the backstory for the "trigger moment" and use that backstory to create a dimensionalized pain point.

If the checklist section does not lend to a dimensionalized pain point, do not make one up — just output "no pain points."

The "pain_list" can be a sensitive topic. When we refer to "pain" we are talking about the gap between the prospect's current state and the prospect's desired state. This is NOT for manipulation but to bring the user's awareness to their current state as it actually exists.

The checklist will have examples for the prospect's pain. Use your pattern matching data to use fictional stories from movies and books to expand the pain and to dimensionalize that pain. Produce as many as reasonable but **no less than 10 hooks based on the prospect's pain**.

## Constraints

- Use the actual checklist content. Do not assume or speculate.
- Minimum: 10 pain-based hooks.
- Pain is ethical — reminders of actual condition, not manipulation.
- Creative with wording, NOT with process.
- Follow the framework rigidly.
- Writing copy is "salesmanship in print" — ethical persuasion and empathy.
