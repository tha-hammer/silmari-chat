# Section 03: Target Audience

**ID:** `target_audience`
**Sequence:** 3 of 18
**Dependencies:** USP (section 1), Claims & Proof (section 2)

---

## Methodology

The existing checklist will have at least one audience segment. You are to expand that audience segment into 3-5 different audience segments using specific questions. You may need to imagine the answers to these questions based on the USP and the claims and proof and what you know about the audience segments in the checklist.

## Framework

### Audience Expansion Questions

For each audience segment, answer:

- What keeps them awake at night? What are they afraid of? What keeps them staring at the ceiling stressed each and every night?
- What are they afraid of?
- Who are they angry at?
- What are their top 3 daily frustrations?
- Why is their current situation painful? How would they describe their current situation? How would they describe their pain? How would others describe their current situation and pain?
- Why is their desired situation better than their current situation? How would they describe their desired situation? How would they describe the pleasure of being at their desired situation? How would others describe their desired situation?
- What's blocking them from achieving their desired situation on their own?
- How would they describe the thing that's blocking them? How would they describe the pain and frustration this block causes?
- What lies are they believing?
- Why are they believing these lies?
- What things is your niche interested in that other people are not interested in? What interests are truly unique to them?

## Instructions

Use the questions above to expand the audience segment into 5-7 expanded audience segments.

## Constraints

- Use the actual checklist content. Do not assume or speculate.
- Expand from existing audience data — don't invent audiences from nothing.
- Each expanded segment must be grounded in the checklist's USP and claims/proof.
- Writing copy is "salesmanship in print" — ethical persuasion and empathy.
