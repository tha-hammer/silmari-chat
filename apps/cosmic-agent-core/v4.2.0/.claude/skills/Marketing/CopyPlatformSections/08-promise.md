## Methodology

Sales copy is "fractal" in nature. The "your appeal" section gives 4 categories of appeal. Each appeal will generate different "mechanisms", "claims and proof", different obstacles for the "what stops them" section. Your job is to reason through these dependencies and produce the actual text, not just a high level description of what might be done. 
CORE FRAMEWORKS:

1. FRACTAL AIDA PATTERN
Each level of writing must follow the AIDA pattern (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action):
- Individual sentences contain micro-AIDA
- Paragraphs contain sentence-level AIDA
- Sections contain paragraph-level AIDA

2. EMOTIONAL-LOGICAL BALANCE
- A (Attention): 80% emotional appeal, 20% logical setup
- I (Interest): 60% emotional hook, 40% logical foundation
- D (Desire): 40% emotional amplification, 60% logical justification
- A (Action): 90% emotional urgency, 10% logical reassurance

ADVERTISEMENT SPECIFIC GUIDELINES:
- Each sentence must be self-contained yet connected
- First sentence: Pattern-interrupt + emotional spike
- Middle sentences: Problem-agitation + solution-hint
- Final sentence: Clear, urgent call-to-action
- Use sensory and emotional language
- Create immediate relevance

## Framework

### Develop Your Promise

A **promise** tells prospects the **big benefit** they gain by consuming the marketing message. NOT the big benefit of your product! The big benefit of **consuming your message** (Webinar, VSL, ad, etc.)

### Key Points
- **Presents a specific outcome and result**
  *Example:* *"Lose 20 lbs in 20 days!"*
- **Conveys your mechanism is different or unique**
- **Solves an urgent problem**
- **Fulfills a deep desire**

### Example Promise
*"Ask These 7 Simple Questions and Watch 50% of the Prospects Who Previously Told You..."*
- "I Need To Think About It"
- "I Need To Talk To Someone Else"
- "I Don't Have The Money"
*"...Suddenly Pay in Full — On The Very First Call!"*

## Instructions

The framework contains the criteria to create promises for the user's offer. Produce as many as reasonable but **no less than 5 big benefits** the prospect will gain by consuming the marketing message. Think step by step how to use the framework and the appropriate checklist elements to write the promises and provide copy examples.

## Constraints

- Use the actual checklist content. Do not assume or speculate.
- Minimum: 5 promises.
- Promises are about consuming the MESSAGE, not the product.
- Creative with wording, NOT with process.
- Follow the framework rigidly.
- Writing copy is "salesmanship in print" — ethical persuasion and empathy.
