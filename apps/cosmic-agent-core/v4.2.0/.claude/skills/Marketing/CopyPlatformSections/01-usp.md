## Framework

### Establish Your USP

Ask yourself:
- **What's the most powerful benefit?**
- **What's the biggest growing trend in your market?**
- **Can you position it uniquely?**
- **Can you make it seem better than the competition?**
- **Can you create a "new category" to make yourself first in mind?**
- **What unfilled needs can you capitalize on?**

### Three Aspects of the USP

1. **Uniqueness** (*Must SEEM or BE unique*)
2. **Usefulness** (*Must have a benefit to the customer*)
3. **Conceptual Simplicity** (*Simple = Trendy*)

### How to Construct the USP

1. **Outline A Problem.** Start with the words "You know how" — then using simple language, outline one to three problems, difficulties, perceptions, dissatisfactions and feelings people might have that you know the solution proposed by the outline can potentially solve.

2. **Outline Your Solution.** Follow #1 with "Well, what I do is" — and briefly outline how you solve the problem by demonstrating HOW what you do helps people solve these problems. No cliches, industry language or abbreviations or making it sound like a sales pitch.
