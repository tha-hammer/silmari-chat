## Methodology

Sales copy is "fractal" in nature. The copy platform gives granular detail and examples on each part of the AIDA framework. For each section in the checklist, expand that section using the copy platform. Reason through nested looped dependencies, nested dependencies, looped dependencies, and linear dependencies. The checklist provides the data IN, the copy platform guides the DO, producing structured OUT.

How to produce the expanded checklist: This checklist will be used as a prompt for an LLM. You must not be vague or offer comments, "best practices", or instructions. Your job is to use the copy platform and the user input to actually fill in the copy platform sections.

## Framework

### Brainstorm a List of Claims

Types of proof:
- **Proof of results**
- **Case studies**
- **Social proof**
- **Mainstream press**
- **Living proof (your own experience)**
- **Peer-reviewed studies**
- **Historical proof**
- **Reason-why logical proof**
- **Story proof**
- **Specificity increases credibility** (e.g., "We made $1,347" vs. "We made thousands")

### Example Claim & Proof

- **Claim:** "We have the highest distribution of sales reps in the industry."
- **Proof:** "Last month we received 1,521 applications, 622 video submissions, 327 interviews, and hired 45 candidates."

- **Claim:** "Our blood test is more relevant to optimal health than the standard blood test."
- **Proof:** "Standard tests are based on the 'average' person. Since the average person over 45 is on at least 2 medications, 'average' is broken at a minimum and a complete joke if you want to be optimal."
