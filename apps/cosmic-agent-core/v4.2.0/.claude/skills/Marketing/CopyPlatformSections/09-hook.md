## Framework

### How to Develop Your Hook

A **hook** is the element of your marketing message that **grabs attention** and gives a reason to continue consuming the message.

### Key Points:
- **Different & Unique**
- **Relevant to wants, needs, desires**
- **Instantly communicates benefit**
- **Conveys newness & freshness**
- **Built on a unique idea**
- **Prevents mental "opt-out" (Not seen before)**

> **Remember:**
> You know what **NOT** to say based on competitors,
> and what **TO** say based on market sophistication.

### Hook Construction Elements
- **Call to audience:** *Did you know / If you / Are you / For ___*
- **Specificity of issue / circumstance / situation**
- **Revelation of a new idea, concept, or vehicle**

### Dissecting an Example

*The 'Post-it Note' trick used by the world's top CEOs and high-achievers to do the work of 3 people with plenty of energy to spare*

Because the human mind communicates in avatars and representations to generate "meaning," examine the parts:

- **"Post-it Note"** has meaning because it is ubiquitous. The prospect can imagine it in use.
- **"world's top CEOs and high-achievers"** has meaning because the prospect likely aspires to be like them and will readily believe they use "Post-it Notes."
- **"do the work of 3 people with plenty of energy to spare"** has meaning because the prospect equates this with making more money, being admired and envied, being superior, being more successful.

Humans react the same, think the same. We need hooks that connect deeply through meaningful, tangible elements the prospect can instantly visualize and relate to their aspirations.
