## Methodology

Sales copy is "fractal" in nature. Reason through nested looped dependencies. The checklist provides data IN, the copy platform guides DO, producing structured OUT.

## Framework

### Why³ (Why Cubed)

ALL marketing must **quickly** answer these three questions:
1. **Why should I listen to you?**
2. **What's in it for me?**
3. **Why now?**

### The Blair Warren Formula

People will do anything for those who encourage their dreams, justify their failures, allay their fears, confirm their suspicions, and help them throw rocks at their enemies.

As you expand the checklist using Why³, apply the Blair Warren formula to structure the answer to each question.

### Example Copy Using This Framework

- **Justify their failures:**
  *"It's not your fault. The real reason you failed is because (external factor beyond their control)."*

- **Allay their fears:**
  *"Closing shouldn't be high-pressure. In fact, the prospect will close themselves when they see you as a leader."*
  *"You're struggling to lose those last 15-20 pounds because you are literally fighting your own body. And you're still fighting your own body because everyone is focused on the wrong things."*

- **Confirm their suspicions:**
  *"Yes, all the good salespeople ARE taken. But the best ones aren't looking for jobs — they're waiting for the right opportunity."*
  *"Yes, diet and exercise ARE important. But even the best diet and exercise program will fail if your hormones are out of whack."*

- **Throw rocks at their enemies:**
  *"You can do this without the old, outdated sales tactics from Grant Cardone or Dan Lok..."*
  *"You don't need another doctor telling you 'it's all in your head' because it's not in your head. It's in your body, it's totally normal, and it's totally treatable."*
