## Methodology

Sales copy is "fractal" in nature. The copy platform gives granular detail and examples on each part of the AIDA framework. Reason through nested looped dependencies. The "your appeal" section is dependent on "your hook," and vice versa — circular dependencies must be resolved. The checklist provides data IN, the copy platform guides DO, producing structured OUT.

You must not be vague or offer comments, "best practices", or instructions. Your job is to use the copy platform and the user input to actually fill in the sections.

## Framework

The mechanism is the proprietary method of executing upon the new opportunity. The biggest, easiest, fastest, safest, most predictable, and only way to capitalize on the new opportunity should be through the mechanism. That's the point.

The mechanism is an explanation of the "reason why" the product or service can actually deliver on the promised benefits and results.

## Instructions

Use the actual checklist. Do not assume.

Think step by step how to create 12-15 mechanisms based on the checklist. Use the claims and proof in combination with the USP to provide copy examples so that when the checklist is sent to an LLM to write copy, the mechanism is presented in a persuasive manner.

You should get creative with the way you justify the prospect's failures or confirm what they suspect to be true but NOT with the process. You are to ONLY use the framework to expand the checklist.

## Constraints

- Use the actual checklist content. Do not assume or speculate.
- Produce 12-15 mechanisms minimum.
- Creative with wording, NOT with process.
- Follow the framework rigidly.

- Pain references serve as reminders of the prospect's actual condition to connect deeper.
