# The Ten Agreements

> The 10 beliefs a prospect must hold before they will buy. Use this as a diagnostic checklist for any piece of copy.

---

## The Framework

Before a prospect converts, they must have reached agreement — consciously or unconsciously — on all 10 of these statements. Any unresolved agreement is a conversion killer.

---

## The 10 Agreements

1. **My dreams can become reality through you.**
   - The prospect must believe your offer can actually produce the outcome they want.
   - Copy task: Paint the vision vividly. Make the transformation feel real and attainable.

2. **The outcome is obviously better.**
   - They must clearly see the contrast between their current state and the desired state.
   - Copy task: Dimensionalize the pain. Make the vision of the future irresistible.

3. **My goals are within reach with your product.**
   - Not just "this is possible" — but "I specifically can achieve this."
   - Copy task: Use audience-specific examples and case studies that mirror their situation.

4. **I can personally attain what I want through your product.**
   - Address the "but that won't work for ME" objection.
   - Copy task: Handle the most common objections before the prospect voices them. Use specificity.

5. **Your product stands out from other choices.**
   - Why you over every alternative (including doing nothing)?
   - Copy task: Differentiation via mechanism. What do you do that no one else does?

6. **I believe your product does what you claim.**
   - Credibility and proof.
   - Copy task: Stack multiple proof types. Specificity creates believability.

7. **The timing is perfect for me right now.**
   - Why now, not later?
   - Copy task: Create urgency (real, not manufactured). Show the cost of delay.

8. **It aligns with my personal timeline.**
   - The result fits their life and schedule.
   - Copy task: Address time objections. Show how your mechanism fits into their existing life.

9. **It's a perfect fit for me, my life, my family, my business.**
   - Personal relevance — "this is FOR me."
   - Copy task: Speak directly to their segment. Use their exact language. Bullseye client profiling.

10. **I trust the source, proof, and case studies.**
    - Trust in you, the company, and the evidence.
    - Copy task: Build credibility through consistency, transparency, and specific testimonials from people like them.

---

## Using This Checklist

**For copy audits:** Read through your finished copy and ask: does this piece create agreement on each of the 10 points? Mark any unresolved agreements — they're objections you haven't handled.

**For copy planning:** Before writing, identify which agreements are likely to be the hardest to earn for this specific audience. Put extra copy weight on those points.

**For headlines and opening copy:** The opening must start earning at least Agreements 1 and 2. If the opening doesn't establish that the outcome is real AND better, the prospect won't continue.
