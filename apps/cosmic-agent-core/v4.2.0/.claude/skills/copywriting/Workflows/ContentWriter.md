# Content Writer Workflow

> Generate final marketing content from a completed copy platform. Produces LinkedIn posts, sales messages, email sequences, and blog articles.

**Prerequisites:** A completed copy platform checklist from `CopyPlatform.md`. If the user doesn't have one, route them to `CopyPlatform.md` first.

**Read before executing:** `../Templates/AIDAFramework.md`, `../Templates/EmotionalTriggers.md`

---

## Content Type Routing

| Request | Format | Length | Primary Framework |
|---------|--------|--------|------------------|
| LinkedIn post | Short-form | 150-300 words | Hook → Pain → Promise → Proof → CTA |
| Sales message | Medium-form | 500-1000 words | Full AIDA + cost of delay |
| Email sequence | 5-email series | 150-400 words/email | Sequential persuasion arc |
| Blog article | Long-form | 1500-3000 words | Three-Act structure |

---

## LinkedIn Posts

**Structure (5 components):**
1. **Hook & Pain Point** (5-10 words) — Pattern interrupt that names the pain
2. **Core Promise** (15-20 words) — What's possible if they keep reading
3. **Mechanism Snapshot** (25-30 words) — How it works (simplified)
4. **Proof Point** (10-15 words) — One specific, credible result
5. **Call to Action** (5-10 words) — One clear next step

**Writing rules:**
- First line must stop the scroll — use a provocative question, bold claim, or pattern interrupt
- No corporate language — write how you speak
- One idea per post
- Specificity beats generality ("$47,000 in 90 days" not "significant revenue")
- End with a question OR a CTA, not both

## Sales Messages

**Structure:**
1. **Cost of Delay** — What's the pain of NOT solving this now?
2. **Market Problem Expansion** — Dimensionalize the problem (make it vivid, specific, unavoidable)
3. **Solution Promise** — The transformation available to them
4. **Claims & Evidence** — 3-5 specific claims with proof
5. **Mechanism Explanation** — How the solution works (the "why it works")
6. **Features/Benefits Matrix** — List features → translate each to prospect benefit
7. **Future State Vision** — Paint the after picture (use Maslow, use sensory detail)
8. **Call to Action** — Clear, specific, low-friction next step

**Emotional-logical balance:**
- Opening: 80% emotional, 20% logical
- Body: 50% emotional, 50% logical
- Close: 80% emotional urgency, 20% logical reassurance

## Email Sequences (5-Email Series)

**Email 1: Welcome & Hook**
- Establish rapport and set expectations
- Introduce the Big Idea
- Open a curiosity loop
- Tone: warm, conversational, not salesy
- CTA: simple engagement (reply, click, read)

**Email 2: Story & Pain Points**
- Tell the origin/transformation story (use `SalesStory.md`)
- Dimensionalize the pain (use pain_list from copy platform)
- Show you understand their world intimately
- No pitch yet
- CTA: continue the story / share their experience

**Email 3: Solution & Mechanism**
- Introduce the mechanism (how your solution works)
- Differentiate from what they've already tried
- Show the Why³ (surface → real → core reason this works)
- Soft introduce the offer
- CTA: learn more / see how it works

**Email 4: Proof & Credibility**
- Lead with a specific result (case study, testimonial, data)
- Stack proof types (social proof + data + story proof)
- Handle the main objection directly
- Build urgency (why now?)
- CTA: take action / book call / claim offer

**Email 5: Urgency & Close**
- Restate the transformation (before/after contrast)
- Vision of the future (10 aspirational outcomes)
- Clear, direct CTA
- Cost of delay (what's lost by waiting)
- Final call to action — no hedging

## Blog Articles (Long-Form)

**Three-Act Structure:**

**Act 1 (25% — Setup):**
- Hook that names the reader's exact situation
- Establish the problem's stakes
- Promise of what the article will deliver
- Plant tension seeds that will resolve in Act 3

**Act 2 (50% — Confrontation):**
- Deep problem exploration (pain dimensionalized)
- Failed solutions they've already tried
- The turning point / new perspective / mechanism
- Escalate emotional investment
- Layer proof (case studies, data, stories)
- Build to the climax

**Act 3 (25% — Resolution):**
- Emotional payoff — the "after" vision
- Logical conclusion — summarize the argument
- Clear next step
- Teachable moment

**Writing rules:**
- Use subheadings as mini-hooks
- Each paragraph should end with a reason to read the next
- Vary sentence length (rhythm matters)
- Use the reader's exact language (not industry jargon)
- One CTA at the end, not scattered throughout

## Iterative Enhancement Process

Apply these 4 phases to any content piece after the first draft:

1. **Initial** — Core elements in place, basic AIDA structure
2. **Enhancement** — Add emotional triggers, urgency, specificity
3. **Refinement** — Strengthen USP, improve flow, optimize for the platform
4. **Polish** — Verify all copy platform elements represented, sharpen CTA

---

## Quality Checklist (Run Before Delivering)

- [ ] Does the opening stop the scroll / grab attention?
- [ ] Is the prospect treated as the hero?
- [ ] Is the pain vivid and specific (not generic)?
- [ ] Is the promise believable and specific?
- [ ] Is there at least one proof point?
- [ ] Is the CTA clear and low-friction?
- [ ] Does it pass the Ten Agreements test? (see `../Templates/TenAgreements.md`)
- [ ] Is it free of corporate clichés, jargon, and hedging?
- [ ] Does the emotional-logical ratio match the content type?
